🎯 WHY THIS MATTERS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

YOUR CURL COMMANDS:
  GET  /embeddings/getEmbeddingModels  → Lists available models
  POST /embeddings/extract             → Converts text → vectors

WHAT EMBEDDINGS DO:
  "Order System"     → [0.2, 0.8, -0.3, ...]  (numbers)
  "Shopping Cart"    → [0.1, 0.7, -0.2, ...]  (similar = close)
  "Weather Service"  → [0.9, -0.5, 0.4, ...]  (different = far)

WHY DEEPWIKI NEEDS THEM:
  Code Files → Embeddings → Vector DB → AI Search → Generate Wiki
  Your Question → Embedding → Find Similar Code → Answer

BENEFITS OF YOUR SERVICE:
  ✅ FREE (no OpenAI costs)
  ✅ FAST (internal network)
  ✅ PRIVATE (data stays in yo-digital.com)
  ✅ STABLE (no Ollama crashes!)
